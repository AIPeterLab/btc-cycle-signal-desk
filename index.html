Exact Rule

  • Signal source is the Bitcoin halving date, with fixed pre-halving and post-halving day counts.
  • Buy / hold BTC from 500 days before the halving date through day 540 after the halving date, inclusive.
  • Hold Cash before the pre-halving buy date and after the day-540 sell date.
  • The confirmed 50-week SMA signal and daily SMA50/SMA200 golden cross are early-entry overlays only; the calendar cycle entry overrides indicator allocation.

Guardrails

  • No QQQ, QLD, SPY, SSO, TQQQ, MACD, EMA crossover, or DCA rules are part of the BTC holding-window signal.
  • The weekly 50-week SMA signal changes only after a completed Sunday weekly close can be used on Monday UTC.
  • Bottom-zone context can help describe the cycle, but it is not an automatic buy signal.
  • This is a rules-based tracking dashboard and is not financial advice.
